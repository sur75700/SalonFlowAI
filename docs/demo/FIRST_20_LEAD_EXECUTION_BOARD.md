# SalonFlow AI — First 20 Lead Execution Board

## Goal

Build and manage the first 20 salon leads for SalonFlow AI pilot/demo outreach.

The goal is not only to collect names. The goal is to move real salon businesses toward demo conversations.

## Success Target

- 20 qualified leads identified
- 10 high-priority messages sent
- 5 follow-ups sent
- 2 demo conversations created
- 1 serious pilot candidate identified

## Lead Segmentation Target

- 8 hair salons
- 4 beauty studios
- 3 nail studios
- 3 brow/lash studios
- 2 barbershops

## Priority Scoring

Score each lead from 1 to 5.

5 = strong fit, active business, many services, visible booking/admin need, likely to care about analytics.

4 = good fit, active salon, clear appointment flow.

3 = possible fit, needs more research.

2 = weak fit, unclear need.

1 = low priority.

## Status Values

- New
- Contacted
- Replied
- Demo Proposed
- Demo Booked
- Demo Completed
- Follow-Up Needed
- Offer Sent
- Pilot Candidate
- Closed Won
- Closed Lost

## Daily Execution Rule

Minimum daily action:

- Add or verify 5 leads
- Send 5 outreach messages
- Send 3 follow-ups
- Try to book 1 demo conversation

## Message Source

Use:

- docs/demo/OUTREACH_MESSAGE_PACK.md
- docs/demo/PILOT_SALES_ONE_PAGER.md
- docs/demo/PRODUCT_DEMO_SCRIPT.md

## Follow-Up Rule

If no reply after 24-48 hours, send one polite follow-up.

If no reply after second follow-up, mark as cold and move forward.

## Demo Booking Rule

When a lead shows interest, move status to Demo Proposed.

When time is agreed, move status to Demo Booked.

After demo, mark Demo Completed and write next action.

## Safety Rules

- Do not spam
- Do not overpromise
- Keep messages respectful
- Track every reply
- Prioritize warm and active businesses
- Stop messaging if the lead says no
