## Ideal Pilot Salon Profile

Best-fit pilot salon:

- Appointment-based business
- Uses Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, phone, or messages for bookings
- Has multiple services
- Has repeat clients
- Needs better organization
- Owner or manager cares about daily revenue visibility
- Team may benefit from Armenian, Russian, English, or French UI
- Could benefit from daily PDF reporting

Good business types:

- Hair salon
- Beauty studio
- Barbershop
- Nail studio
- Brow/lash studio
- Massage or spa studio

## Lead Qualification Checklist

Score each lead from 1 to 5.

### Score 5

Strong fit:

- Active business
- Clear appointment workflow
- Multiple services
- Visible online presence
- Likely booking/admin complexity
- Owner or manager is reachable

### Score 4

Good fit:

- Active salon
- Has services and client flow
- Contact channel visible
- May benefit from better operations

### Score 3

Possible fit:

- Some appointment activity visible
- Needs more research

### Score 2

Weak fit:

- Low activity
- Unclear contact
- Unclear appointment need

### Score 1

Low priority:

- Not relevant
- Not active
- No clear way to contact

## Pilot Offer

Offer a small controlled pilot.

Pilot includes:

- Product walkthrough
- Basic salon workflow setup
- Client/service/appointment demo data review
- Owner/operator training session
- Feedback collection
- Improvement list after first usage review

Pilot goal:

Prove whether SalonFlow AI helps the salon manage daily operations more clearly from one workspace.

## Pilot Fit Questions

Ask:

- How do you currently manage bookings?
- Where do you keep client information?
- How do you manage services, prices, and durations?
- Do you track daily revenue?
- Do daily PDF reports help your workflow?
- Which language would your team prefer?
- What is the biggest missing feature for your salon?
- Would you test this with sample data?

## Feedback Questions

After demo, ask:

- Was the workflow clear?
- Which screen was most useful?
- Which screen was least useful?
- Was the language support useful?
- Would your team use this daily?
- What would need to change before a pilot?
- Would you like to test it for a short pilot period?

## Outcome Tracking

Possible outcomes:

### Warm Lead

Next action:

- Follow up in 1-3 days

### Demo Booked

Time is agreed.

Next action:

- Prepare command center and monitoring checklist

### Pilot Candidate

The salon wants to test.

Next action:

- Define pilot scope and feedback date

### Not Interested

The salon declines.

Next action:

- Respectfully close and stop messaging

### No Reply

No response after follow-ups.

Next action:

- Mark cold and move to next lead

## Safety Rules

- Do not spam
- Do not overpromise
- Do not claim Android real-device QA is passed until real-device QA is completed
- Do not claim iOS/TestFlight is live until Apple/TestFlight setup is done
- Do not expose tokens
- Do not show .env files
- Do not change production settings during demo
- Do not send too many follow-ups
- Stop if the lead says no
