# SalonFlow AI — Demo Close & Follow-Up Pack

## Purpose

This pack explains how to handle a salon lead after they reply, accept a demo, or finish a demo call.

The goal is to keep the process clear, professional, respectful, and focused on the next step.

## Demo Call Goal

The demo call is not only to show screens.

The real goal is to understand the salon workflow, show how SalonFlow AI fits, and agree on a clear next action.

## Demo Call Structure

### 1. Opening

Thank you for taking the time. I will quickly show how SalonFlow AI helps organize bookings, clients, services, analytics, and reports in one workspace.

### 2. Discovery Questions

- How do you currently manage bookings?
- Where do you keep client records?
- How do you manage services, prices, and durations?
- Do you currently track completed revenue or upcoming scheduled value?
- What is the most annoying admin problem in your daily workflow?

### 3. Product Walkthrough

Show in this order:

1. Login and protected workspace
2. Dashboard
3. Clients
4. Services
5. Appointments
6. Analytics
7. Reports

### 4. Business Relevance

Connect every screen to a real business outcome:

- Dashboard -> daily visibility
- Clients -> organized customer records
- Services -> controlled prices and durations
- Appointments -> clearer booking workflow
- Analytics -> revenue and pipeline visibility
- Reports -> owner-ready summary

### 5. Security Proof

Mention that production analytics is protected:

- Without token -> 401 Unauthorized
- With login token -> 200 OK and AMD analytics data

## Strong Close Questions

- Does this workflow match the way your salon operates?
- Which part would be most useful for your team?
- Would you like to test this as a pilot with your salon workflow?
- Should we choose a small pilot setup as the next step?

## Package Recommendation Logic

Starter:

- simple salon
- small team
- basic booking and client organization need

Professional:

- active salon
- multiple services
- owner needs analytics and reporting
- best default recommendation

Premium:

- larger or more advanced setup
- stronger customization/support need
- more serious operational control requirement

The main value of SalonFlow AI is that it brings bookings, clients, services, analytics, and reports into one workspace instead of scattered tools.

If this still feels useful, we can move forward with a small pilot step.

## Close Rules

- Do not pressure
- Do not overpromise
- Always summarize the business problem
- Recommend the next step clearly
- Track every demo result
- Follow up respectfully
- Stop if the lead says no
