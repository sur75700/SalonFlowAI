Score each lead from 1 to 5.

5 = strong fit, active business, multiple services, visible booking/admin need, likely to care about analytics and reports.

4 = good fit, active business, clear appointment workflow, contact channel visible.

3 = possible fit, needs more research.

2 = weak fit, unclear workflow or low visible activity.

1 = low priority or not relevant.

## Execution Checklist

Before outreach:

- Review the lead profile.
- Confirm the business is active.
- Select the correct message style.
- Do not send duplicate messages.
- Track date/time after sending.
- Do not overpromise.
- Stop if the lead says no.

After sending:

- Update status to Contacted.
- Fill Last Contact date.
- Set follow-up date after 24-48 hours.
- Track any reply.
- If interested, move to Demo Proposed.
- If a time is agreed, move to Demo Booked.
