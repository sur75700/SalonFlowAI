# SalonFlow AI — Incoming Lead Reply Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow explains what to do when a friend replies with a salon, beauty studio, nail studio, or barbershop lead.

The goal is to turn incoming introductions into clean, verified, respectful outreach actions.

## When To Use This

Use this workflow when someone sends:

- Business name
- Instagram link
- Facebook page
- Website
- Google Maps listing
- Public business contact page

## Main Rule

Only use public business information.

Do not store private personal data unless the person clearly shared it for business contact purposes.

## Step 1 — Capture The Reply

When a friend replies, write down:

- Who replied
- What business they suggested
- Public link if available
- Business type
- City/area
- Any useful context

## Step 2 — Verify Public Source

Before adding the lead, verify that the business has at least one public source:

- Instagram business profile
- Facebook business page
- Official website
- Google Maps listing

If there is no public source, leave contact fields blank and mark the lead as Needs Verification.

## Step 3 — Add To Lead Capture Session

Update:

docs/demo/leads/REAL_LEAD_CAPTURE_SESSION_01.md

Add the lead into the lead table with:

- Business Name
- Type
- City/Area
- Public Source
- Channel
- Contact Link
- Score
- Status
- Next Action
- Notes

## Step 4 — Score The Lead

Score from 1 to 5:

5 = strong fit, active business, many services, likely booking/admin/reporting need.

4 = good fit, clear appointment-based business.

3 = possible fit, needs more research.

2 = weak fit.

1 = low priority.

## Step 5 — Choose Message Type

Use:

docs/demo/OUTREACH_MESSAGE_PACK.md

Choose:

- Instagram DM Short for simple first contact
- Instagram DM Pain Point for active salons with visible workflow need
- Facebook Professional for business pages
- Email Professional for website contact forms
- WhatsApp Professional only if the business publicly lists WhatsApp

## Step 6 — Update Intro Tracker

Update:

docs/demo/leads/INTRO_REQUEST_TRACKER_01.md

Change the friend row:

- Reply: Received
- Next Action: Add lead / verify / outreach
- Notes: business name or public link

## Step 7 — Outreach Decision

Before sending outreach, ask:

- Is the business relevant?
- Is the public page active?
- Does it look appointment-based?
- Is the message respectful and short?
- Are we avoiding spam?

If yes, send one respectful message manually.

## Step 8 — After Sending

Update lead status:

- New -> Contacted

Update daily result:

- Outreach messages sent: +1
- Next follow-up date: 24-48 hours later

## Reply Handling

If lead replies positively:

- Status -> Replied
- Send demo preview or ask if they want a short walkthrough
- If interested, Status -> Demo Proposed

If lead asks for details:

- Send PILOT_SALES_ONE_PAGER.md summary

If lead says not interested:

- Status -> Closed Lost
- Stop messaging

## Safety Rules

- Do not spam
- Do not pressure friends
- Do not pressure business owners
- Do not overpromise
- Use only public business info
- Stop if the lead says no
- Track every action
