## Full Description Draft

SalonFlow AI is a premium salon operations platform designed to help salon owners manage daily workflow with clarity and control.

The app brings core salon operations into one clean workspace:

- Dashboard overview
- Booking and appointment management
- Client registry
- Service catalog
- Analytics and business insights
- Daily PDF reports
- Multilingual interface

SalonFlow AI supports English, Armenian, Russian, and French, helping salon teams work in the language that fits their business.

The product is designed for modern salons that want a calm, organized, and professional command center for daily operations.
