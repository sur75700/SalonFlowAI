# SalonFlow AI — Privacy Policy Draft

## Status

Draft prepared for store-readiness.

Final legal review recommended before public launch.

## Product

SalonFlow AI is a salon operations platform for managing bookings, clients, services, analytics, reports, and multilingual business workflows.

## Data We May Collect

SalonFlow AI may process the following data depending on account setup and product usage:

- account login information
- salon business information
- client names
- client phone numbers
- client email addresses if entered
- appointment / booking records
- service names, prices, durations, and status
- operational notes entered by the user
- analytics and report data generated from salon activity

## How Data Is Used

Data is used to:

- provide salon management functionality
- authenticate authorized users
- manage bookings, clients, and services
- generate analytics and reports
- support multilingual product workflows
- improve operational reliability and user experience

## Authentication

SalonFlow AI uses protected login/session flows.

Protected areas such as analytics and report exports require authentication.

## PDF Reports

SalonFlow AI may generate PDF reports from salon operational data.

Reports may include booking, client, service, revenue, or daily summary information depending on the selected report.

## Data Sharing

SalonFlow AI does not intentionally sell user data.

Data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers used to operate the product, such as hosting, database, authentication, or analytics infrastructure.

## Data Storage

Data may be stored in backend infrastructure and database systems used by SalonFlow AI.

Production backend currently runs on Render infrastructure.

Database storage currently uses MongoDB-based infrastructure.

## User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for entering accurate business/client information and for having the appropriate permission to store client-related data in SalonFlow AI.

## Data Security

SalonFlow AI uses protected backend routes and authenticated access for sensitive product areas.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but SalonFlow AI is designed to avoid exposing secrets, tokens, or protected data in public documentation or screenshots.

## Data Deletion

A future public release should provide a clear support/contact process for requesting data correction or deletion.

Before public launch, define the official support email and deletion request process.

## Children

SalonFlow AI is intended for salon/business operations and is not designed for children.
