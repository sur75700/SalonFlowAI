## Logo Direction

The logo should be simple enough to work as an app icon.

Recommended concepts:

### Concept A — Royal Flow Monogram

A clean SF monogram with a subtle flow curve, gold accent, and deep cosmic background.

### Concept B — Salon Command Star

A minimal star/spark mark combined with a soft flow line, representing intelligence and business clarity.

### Concept C — Elegant AI Orbit

A circular orbit around a small salon/flow symbol, suggesting AI-powered daily operations.

### Concept D — Premium Calendar Flow

A subtle calendar/check/flow icon combined with a royal glow, representing bookings and daily salon management.

## What The Logo Should Avoid

Avoid:

- too many details
- tiny text inside the icon
- realistic scissors or combs as the main identity
- cluttered AI robot imagery
- generic chatbot logo
- unreadable gradients
- overused neon cyberpunk style
- too much gold that reduces clarity
- low contrast
- unofficial app store badges inside the icon
